# LumaVale – Game Pillars

## Purpose

Game Pillars define the core experience of LumaVale.

Every feature, mechanic, UI, NPC, map, and future update should support at least one of these pillars.

If a feature supports none of them, it should not be included in the MVP.

---

# Pillar 1 — Living World

## Vision

The world feels alive even when the player does nothing.

## Design Principles

- NPCs follow daily schedules.
- Monsters respawn naturally.
- Resources regenerate over time.
- Day and night affect the environment.
- World events happen automatically.
- The world continues evolving while players are offline.

## Examples

- Farmer leaves home every morning.
- Merchant closes the shop at night.
- Rain increases mushroom spawn.
- A caravan arrives every evening.

---

# Pillar 2 — Meaningful Exploration

## Vision

Every location should reward curiosity.

Exploration is not only about discovering new maps, but also finding useful resources, hidden events, and opportunities.

## Design Principles

- Every zone has unique resources.
- Every zone has unique risks.
- Every zone has a clear purpose.
- Exploration should always feel rewarding.

## Examples

Forest

- Wood
- Herbs
- Slimes

Mine

- Iron Ore
- Crystal
- PvP Risk

Dungeon

- Boss
- Rare Loot
- Crafting Materials

---

# Pillar 3 — Flexible Adventure

## Vision

Players choose how they want to play.

The game should always remain enjoyable whether playing alone or with others.

## Design Principles

- Solo is fully supported.
- Co-op improves efficiency but is optional.
- PvP is always voluntary.
- No feature requires a fixed team size.

## Examples

Solo

- Quest
- Gathering
- Crafting
- Dungeon with NPC companions

Co-op

- Faster farming
- Harder bosses
- Shared rewards

PvP

- Duel
- Conflict Zone
- World Events

# MVP Scope Rule

The MVP focuses only on features that strengthen these three pillars.

Anything that does not directly improve the player experience around these pillars is postponed until after the MVP.
