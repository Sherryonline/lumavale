# LumaVale – Product Vision

## 1. Elevator Pitch

LumaVale is a cozy multiplayer living-world RPG where players explore, gather resources, fight monsters, craft equipment, and shape a small world that continues evolving even when they are offline. The game is designed to be enjoyable whether playing alone or with friends.

---

## 2. Target Player

Primary Audience

- Players aged 18–35
- Enjoy relaxing progression games
- Like RPGs, life simulation, and cooperative gameplay
- Prefer short gaming sessions (5–30 minutes)

Secondary Audience

- Players who enjoy collecting items
- Players interested in persistent online worlds
- Casual gamers with limited daily playtime

---

## 3. Player Fantasy

The player feels like:

> "I live in a small fantasy town where my actions matter. The world keeps changing, NPCs have their own lives, and every play session helps my character grow."

---

## 4. Unique Selling Point (USP)

Instead of creating a huge MMORPG, LumaVale focuses on a **small but living world**.

Key differentiators:

- NPCs follow daily routines
- The world changes over time
- Solo and multiplayer are equally enjoyable
- No waiting for a full party
- Dynamic world events
- Short, meaningful play sessions

---

## 5. Game Pillars

### Pillar 1 — Living World

NPCs work, travel, sleep, trade, and react to world events.

---

### Pillar 2 — Meaningful Progression

Every session rewards the player through experience, resources, crafting, or discoveries.

---

### Pillar 3 — Play Your Way

Players can enjoy the game solo, cooperatively, or through optional PvP without being forced into any mode.

---

## 6. Non-Goals (Not Included in MVP)

The MVP will NOT include:

- Guild system
- Housing
- Large open world
- Mounts
- Trading between players
- Auction House
- Complex crafting trees
- Voice chat
- Mobile version
- Hundreds of skills
- Hundreds of monsters
- Dozens of maps

---

## 7. Success Metrics

The MVP is considered successful if:

- New players understand the game within 5 minutes.
- Average play session lasts more than 15 minutes.
- Players complete at least one quest.
- Players return the next day.
- Multiplayer sessions run without major synchronization issues.

---

## 8. MVP Boundaries

Playable Class

Combat

NPCs

- Merchant

Monsters

Core Systems
